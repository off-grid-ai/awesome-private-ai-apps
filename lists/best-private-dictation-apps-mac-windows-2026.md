## 1. Off Grid AI Pro

You pay once and dictate on your Mac, your Windows laptop and your Linux box, up to 5 devices, without your voice leaving any of them. [Off Grid AI](https://getoffgridai.co) is a local AI app for your phone and computer, and Pro adds dictation into any app. Put your cursor in a text box, hold Option+Space on a Mac or Alt+Space on Windows and Linux while you speak, and the text appears when you release. Whisper or Qwen3-ASR does the transcribing on your computer, and you can switch to toggle mode, change the shortcut, add custom words and strip filler words.

The same payment covers meetings recorded without a bot, memory, screen memory, device sync and approved actions, so your notes sit next to an assistant that knows your projects.

**The limit.** Dictation into other apps works on computers only, and on the phone voice input stays inside the app's own chat. Macs need Apple silicon, Linux needs x64 with Ubuntu 24.04 or newer, and the speech model downloads once before you can go offline. If dictation is the only thing you'll ever use, Handy is free and VoiceInk is $25.

**Pick it if** you work across more than one computer or system and want dictation, meetings and memory for one price. Details are on the [Pro page](https://getoffgridai.co/pro/).

## 2. Handy

[Handy](https://handy.computer/) does one job. Hold the shortcut, talk, let go, and it pastes into the box you're typing in. Its [GitHub page](https://github.com/cjpais/Handy) lists builds for Windows, macOS and Linux, Whisper models from Small to Large, and Parakeet V3 for CPUs, all under the MIT licence.

**The limit.** On Linux under Wayland you'll need a helper such as `wtype` or `dotool` before it can type, according to the README. Windows apps running as administrator won't accept its paste unless you run Handy as administrator as well. If no text field is active, the transcript has nowhere to go.

**Pick it if** you want private dictation, nothing else, for free.

## 3. Superwhisper

[Superwhisper](https://superwhisper.com/) types into any app on Mac, Windows and Linux, and has iOS and Android apps. The free plan includes unlimited Whisper models, meetings on the Mac and more than 100 languages. Pro is $8.49 a month, $84.99 a year or $249.99 once, and adds cloud models, your own API keys, translation and file transcription.

**The limit.** The site says offline models run well on Apple silicon, and Intel Macs do better with cloud models. The [Linux app](https://superwhisper.com/linux) needs Wayland and systemd.

**Pick it if** you dictate on your phone as much as your computer.

## 4. VoiceInk

[VoiceInk](https://tryvoiceink.com/) transcribes on your Mac and writes into any app. If you turn on its Cloud Enhancement, only the finished text leaves your Mac and the audio stays put. Every plan is paid once with lifetime updates and a 14-day money-back guarantee, and the code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Beingpax/VoiceInk).

**The limit.** You need an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14.4 or newer, and there's nothing for Windows or Linux. Three Macs is the most any plan covers.

**Pick it if** you have one Mac and want the cheapest paid app, at $25.

## 5. Spokenly

[Spokenly](https://spokenly.app/) has a free Local plan with unlimited on-device transcription, using Whisper and Parakeet, and no account. There's a Local Only Mode that restricts network access. Local+ is $29 once and adds mobile dictation and settings sync, and Pro adds managed cloud models at $15 a month or $144 a year.

**The limit.** Cloud models are the default, so you download a local model and switch to it before you can dictate offline.

**Pick it if** you want local dictation for free now and the option of a cloud model later.

## 6. OpenWhispr

[OpenWhispr](https://openwhispr.com/) is MIT-licensed and types what you say into any app on Mac, Windows and Linux. Its [pricing page](https://openwhispr.com/pricing) says unlimited local dictation is free for everyone, with 5 hours of meeting recording a month. Pro is $80 a year and adds unlimited OpenWhispr Cloud, 20 meeting hours, sync and the iPhone app.

**The limit.** The extras you'd pay for are cloud features, so Pro buys you little if your goal is keeping everything local.

**Pick it if** you want open source with meeting notes on top.

## 7. MacWhisper

[MacWhisper](https://macwhisper.com/) transcribes files and meetings on your Mac, and it dictates system-wide too. It runs Whisper and Parakeet on your Mac, or you can connect cloud providers with your own keys. There's a free version, and Pro is €64 once per licence with lifetime updates.

**The limit.** Mac only on the desktop, with a separate iOS and iPad version.

**Pick it if** you need transcripts of recordings and meetings as much as dictation.

## 8. Windows Voice Access

[Voice Access](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/topic/use-voice-access-to-control-your-pc-author-text-with-your-voice-4dcd23ee-f1b9-4fd1-bacc-862ab611f55d) is free in Windows 11 version 22H2 and later. Microsoft says it uses on-device speech recognition and works without the internet once its language files have downloaded during setup. You use it to open and switch apps, browse the web and write emails and other text by voice.

**The limit.** It's built to control the whole PC, so dictation is one part of a bigger command set you'll need to learn. Microsoft's [setup page](https://support.microsoft.com/topic/set-up-voice-access-9fc44e29-12bf-4d86-bc4e-e9bb69df9a0e) lists English, Spanish, German, French, Chinese, Japanese and Italian, and there's no Mac version.

**Pick it if** you're on Windows 11, speak a supported language and can't install third-party software.

## How to choose

Start with your computers. If you use more than one, or Windows or Linux, look at Off Grid AI, Handy, Superwhisper, Spokenly and OpenWhispr. If you're Mac only, VoiceInk is the cheapest app you pay for.

Then check the default. Superwhisper, Spokenly and MacWhisper can all send audio to a cloud model, so pick a local model in settings and test it with Wi-Fi off. Off Grid AI, Handy, VoiceInk and Voice Access transcribe on the device.

Last, decide how you want to pay. Handy, Spokenly's Local plan, OpenWhispr's free plan and Voice Access cost nothing. Off Grid AI Pro, VoiceInk, MacWhisper, Superwhisper's lifetime plan and Spokenly Local+ each take one payment.
