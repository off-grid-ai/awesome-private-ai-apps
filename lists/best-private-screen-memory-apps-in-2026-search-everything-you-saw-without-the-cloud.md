## 1. Off Grid AI Pro

[Off Grid AI](https://getoffgridai.co) is a free app for chat, images and documents on iOS, Android, macOS (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux. Pro adds screen memory on the computer. Once you press Resume capture, it saves sampled screen moments on your disk and reads them with a local vision model, so you can ask what Sam said about the rollout and get the source. You can exclude apps, pause and delete moments. Reflect shows your day or week by project, app and person, and the heavy reading can run overnight, 3:00 to 8:00 by default, so your computer stays fast while you work. The same Pro records meetings without a bot, and it's one payment of $34.99 right now (regular $119) or $7.99 a month for up to 5 devices.

Your computer has to run a small vision model, and nothing is captured while the app is closed. Pick it if you want Rewind back on Mac, Windows or Linux, kept on your own disk and paid for once.

## 2. Screenpipe

[Screenpipe](https://github.com/screenpipe/screenpipe) records your screen, audio and app events to your disk, transcribes with Whisper, and can use Ollama for answers on your device. It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux, and its API and MCP server let Claude, Cursor and your own scripts read your history. It has 22.4k GitHub stars. The free plan limits searchable history, and full history costs $21 a month or $250 a year, per its [pricing page](https://screenpipe.com/pricing). The code is public under Screenpipe's own source-available licence, and commercial use needs a paid licence. Plan for about 30 GB of disk a month. Pick it if you write code and want raw history for your own agents.

## 3. Windows Recall

Recall saves encrypted snapshots every few seconds and searches them on the PC. Microsoft's [support page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/retrace-your-steps-with-recall-aa03f8a0-a78b-4b3e-b0a1-2eb8ac48701c) says snapshots stay on your drive, aren't shared with Microsoft, and need Windows Hello every time you open them. It filters passwords and card numbers by default and is off until you turn it on. The catch is hardware. You need a Copilot+ PC with a 40 TOPS NPU, 16 GB of RAM and 50 GB free, and it's Windows only. Pick it if you already own that PC.

## 4. Trailback

[Trailback](https://trailback.app) saves the web pages, files, screenshots and copied text you use, then lets you search by describing what you remember, in 10 languages. Its site says it never records your screen or audio, skips passwords and card numbers, and goes online only for licence checks and updates. It's a one-time licence per computer, listed at $9 down from $19 when I checked. Pick it if what you usually lose is a page or a document.

## 5. Windrecorder

[Windrecorder](https://github.com/yuka-friends/Windrecorder) records your screen in small video files and searches them by OCR text, with optional image search and LLM tag summaries. Everything runs locally with no internet needed. It's free under GPL-2.0, has 4k GitHub stars and got a commit on 25 September 2026. Setup uses batch installers and Python, so it's less polished than a store app. Pick it if you're on Windows without a Copilot+ PC and want it free.

## 6. OpenRecall

[OpenRecall](https://github.com/openrecall/openrecall) takes regular screenshots, reads them with local OCR and gives you semantic search, on Windows, macOS and Linux. It's free under AGPL-3.0 and can store data on an encrypted removable disk. You install it with pip and Python 3.11, there are no packaged releases, and the last commit was on 24 September 2025. Pick it if you're comfortable in a terminal and want free on a Mac or Linux.

## What happened to Rewind

Rewind was the app most people meant by "remember everything I saw". Meta bought Limitless, the company behind it, and [Limitless says](https://www.limitless.ai/) Rewind's screen and audio capture stopped on 19 December 2025. Old recordings stay readable in its apps through 2026. If you're still on Rewind, export what you need and move to one of the apps above.

## How to choose

Check your hardware first. A Copilot+ PC makes Recall the easy answer, and an older PC or a Mac rules it out. Then decide what you lose most often. If it's whole screens and conversations, you want a screen recorder such as Off Grid AI, Screenpipe or Windrecorder. If it's links and files, Trailback is cheaper and lighter. Last, count the disk and the cost. Continuous capture fills gigabytes each month, and Screenpipe's full history is $750 over three years against a one-time price for Off Grid AI or Trailback.

## FAQ

### Is there a private Rewind alternative that doesn't use the cloud?

Yes. Off Grid AI Pro, Screenpipe, Windrecorder, OpenRecall and Windows Recall all keep captures on your computer. Trailback stays offline too, but it saves pages, files and copied text instead of recording the screen.

### What happened to Rewind AI?

Meta bought Limitless, the company behind Rewind. Rewind stopped capturing on 19 December 2025, and earlier recordings stay readable through 2026.

### Can I use Windows Recall without a Copilot+ PC?

No. Recall needs a Copilot+ PC with a 40 TOPS NPU and 16 GB of RAM. On other PCs, Off Grid AI Pro, Screenpipe, Windrecorder and OpenRecall all run on Windows.

### Is there a free local screen memory app for Mac?

Yes. Screenpipe has a free plan with limited history, and OpenRecall is free and open source. Off Grid AI's core app is free, but screen memory needs Pro.

### How much disk space does screen memory use?

It depends on the app and your settings. Screenpipe estimates about 30 GB a month, with retention you can change. Recall needs 50 GB free to turn on and pauses when free space drops below 25 GB.
