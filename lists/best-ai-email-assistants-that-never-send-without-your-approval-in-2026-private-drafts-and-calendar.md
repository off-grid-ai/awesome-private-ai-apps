## 1. Off Grid AI Pro

**What it is.** OGAD is free and open source. Pro connects your Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts) and your Microsoft account (Outlook, Calendar, OneDrive). Those connections only read: Gmail search, Outlook search and read, and calendar lists. Sign-in and mail traffic go straight from your computer to Google or Microsoft, and a local model reads what comes back.

**Drafts, sending and events.** Ask in Chat for a draft and you get a draft in Chat. If you'd rather find it in Gmail, Web Use works in your own signed-in Gmail tab and writes the draft there, through the Off Grid AI extension in Chrome or Firefox or in OGAD's own browser. Ask it to send and it sends, through Apple Mail on a Mac or Outlook on Windows, straight away because you asked. The same goes for events: Apple Calendar on a Mac, Outlook events and tasks on Windows, and Google Calendar through Web Use.

**What waits for your yes.** Anything Off Grid AI suggests on its own, such as a follow-up email after a call or a Linear issue, waits in **Actions** with the source it came from. A routine never changes anything without your approval. If the request comes from a paired browser, sending an email and creating or changing events wait for approval in OGAD. Web Use hands passwords, codes, CAPTCHAs and card numbers back to you in code, and it hands you steps you can't undo.

**Best for.** Client, legal or personal mail you don't want read by any AI company.

**Price.** $34.99 once for lifetime access, a limited-time price down from $119, or $7.99 a month. No account.

**The catch.** Chat runs what you ask for straight away, so ask for a draft when you want a draft. The connectors read Google's primary calendar only, and on Linux there's no mail or calendar tool. Reading mail needs the internet, and Google setup can ask you to create your own Google Cloud sign-in client. OGAD runs on Mac (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux.
