## Handy

**What it is.** A free, open-source dictation app under the MIT licence. Press the shortcut, speak, let go, and Handy pastes the text into the app you're in. Its site says your voice stays on your computer. It runs Whisper and Parakeet offline, and builds ship for Mac (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux. It keeps a transcript history and a dictionary, and it can run an AI clean-up pass before pasting.

**Best for.** Anyone who wants private dictation and nothing else, at no cost. It also runs on Intel Macs, which VoiceInk and Off Grid AI don't.

**The catch.** Its README says Wayland support on Linux is limited and needs extra tools such as `wtype`. Its pages don't list file transcription, meetings or a phone app.

## VoiceInk

**What it is.** An open-source (GPL v3) dictation app for Apple silicon Macs. Its site says all transcription happens on your Mac, and only text goes out if you turn on Cloud Enhancement. It adds a personal dictionary, AI clean-up of filler words and a voice assistant mode.

**Best for.** One person on one Apple silicon Mac who wants dictation with filler-word clean-up for $25 once.

**The catch.** It doesn't run on Windows, Linux or Intel Macs. The $25 licence covers one Mac, and three Macs is the most any plan covers.

## Superwhisper

**What it is.** A dictation app for Mac, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android. The free plan includes unlimited offline Whisper models and meetings on the Mac. Pro adds cloud models, your own API keys, translation and audio and video file transcription.

**Best for.** People who dictate on their phone as much as their computer, and who want AI modes that reshape the text.

**The catch.** Its FAQ says offline models only run really well on Apple silicon Macs, and meetings are Mac only. Linux needs Wayland and systemd. Cloud models mean your audio is processed off your computer, so pick an offline model to keep it on your machine.

## Off Grid AI Pro

**What it is.** Dictation inside Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD). Hold **Option+Space** on a Mac, or **Alt+Space** on Windows and Linux, speak, and let go. The words land where your cursor is. In **Voice settings** you choose **Hold**, **Toggle** or **Both**, pick the engine (Whisper, Parakeet or Qwen3-ASR), add custom words, turn on **Remove filler words** or **Format for the app**, and set **Auto-delete** for old recordings.

**Best for.** People who talk all day across more than one computer and want one payment to cover it. **Feed to memory** pulls people, projects and to-dos out of what you say, so a note you dictate after a call joins what Off Grid AI knows about that client. The same key records meetings without a bot and syncs your work to your phone.

**The catch.** Dictation into other apps is desktop only, and Macs need Apple silicon. Linux needs x64 and Ubuntu 24.04 or newer. Models need internet once to download, and local speed depends on your computer. The free app transcribes files and takes voice in chat, but dictation into other apps is Pro.

## FAQ

**What is the best dictation app for Mac or Windows that types into any app and keeps my voice on the computer?**
If you want to pay once and get more than dictation, Off Grid AI Pro. Hold Option+Space on a Mac or Alt+Space on Windows and Linux, speak, and the text lands at your cursor, transcribed on your computer. It is $34.99 once for up to 5 devices, and the same key covers meetings, memory, Sync and Vault. If you only want dictation and pay nothing, pick Handy.

**Is Handy really free?**
Yes. Handy is free and open source under the MIT licence, on Mac, Windows and Linux. It runs Whisper and Parakeet on your computer. Its own pages don't list file transcription or meeting recording.

**Does VoiceInk work on Windows or Linux?**
No. VoiceInk's site says it works only on Apple silicon Macs with macOS 14.4 or later. There is a separate iOS version.

**Does Superwhisper send my voice to the cloud?**
Only when you pick a cloud model. Offline models are free with no word limit. On Linux, the free plan also gives 10,000 cloud words every 30 days. Superwhisper's FAQ says offline models only run really well on Apple silicon Macs.

**Which dictation app costs the least over three years?**
Handy, at $0. VoiceInk is $25 to $49 once, for one to three Macs. Superwhisper Pro is $254.97 over three yearly plans, or $249.99 for lifetime. Off Grid AI Pro is $34.99 once for up to 5 devices.

**Can I dictate into other apps on my phone with Off Grid AI?**
No. Dictation into other apps is on computers. The Off Grid AI phone app takes voice input inside its own chat. Superwhisper has iOS and Android apps, and VoiceInk has an iOS app.
