## 1. Off Grid AI Pro

[Off Grid AI](https://getoffgridai.co) is a free app for chat, images and documents on iOS, Android, macOS (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux. Pro adds Meetings on the computer. It records two tracks, the call's audio and your microphone, so no bot joins Zoom, Teams or Meet. When you stop, a Whisper model writes the transcript on your computer and a local model writes the summary, so the call never reaches an AI company. It tells speakers apart by voice, and you can rename them and ask questions about the call. The same Pro adds a memory of your screens and documents and dictation into any app, and it costs one payment of $34.99 right now (regular $119) or $7.99 a month for up to 5 devices.

Your computer does the work, so it has to run a speech model. There are no shared team spaces, and the free app doesn't include Meetings. Pick it if your calls are client, legal or medical work, you're on Linux, or you'd rather pay once than every month.

## 2. MacWhisper

[MacWhisper](https://macwhisper.com) records Zoom, Teams, Webex, Discord and other calls without a bot, spots when a meeting starts and ends, and transcribes with local models on your Mac. The free version is free forever and Pro is €64 once. It's a transcription tool, so you get a transcript rather than a running memory of your meetings. Pick it if you're on a Mac and want private transcripts for little or no money.

## 3. Granola

Granola listens to your computer's audio and writes notes next to the ones you type, with no bot on any [plan](https://www.granola.ai/pricing). It works in any meeting app and has phone apps for in-person talks. The free plan keeps 30 days of history. Its [security page](https://www.granola.ai/security) says transcription uses providers like Deepgram and AssemblyAI, summaries go to providers like OpenAI and Anthropic, and notes live in a US-hosted AWS cloud. Pick it if you want polished shared notes and you're fine with the cloud.

## 4. Krisp

Krisp started as noise cancellation and still does that better than anything here. Its [meeting assistant](https://krisp.ai/ai-meeting-assistant/) has no bot by default and transcribes English on your device. The other 15 languages go to its servers. Core is $8 a month billed yearly or $16 monthly after a 7-day [trial](https://krisp.ai/pricing/). Pick it if background noise is the bigger problem.

## 5. Built into Zoom and Google Meet

**Zoom.** Zoom's [June 2026 announcement](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/zoom-ai-companion-zoommate/), which renamed AI Companion features as ZoomMate, says eligible paid Workplace plans keep the core AI features at no extra cost. The free Basic plan gets meeting summaries for 3 hosted meetings a month, per Zoom's [ZoomMate page](https://www.zoom.com/en/ai-assistant/). There's no bot, but your admin has to allow it and everyone in the call can see it's on. If you'd rather keep the audio, Zoom's [local recording](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0063640) works on free and paid accounts in the desktop app on Windows, macOS and Linux. Transcribe that file in Off Grid AI or MacWhisper and it never touches a cloud.

**Google Meet.** [Take notes for me](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754931) writes a Google Doc into the organizer's Drive and attaches it to the calendar event. It needs an eligible Workspace edition or Google AI plan, handles 8 languages, and Meet tells every participant it's running. Plain [transcripts](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/12849897) come with Business Standard and up. Neither helps when another company hosts the call on Zoom.

## 6. Fireflies

Fireflies' desktop app for Mac and Windows can [capture system audio](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/6666374717-how-to-record-meetings-without-a-bot-on-the-fireflies-desktop-app) instead of sending its notetaker bot. In that mode it doesn't save the audio file, and transcripts land in your Fireflies web dashboard. There's a [free plan](https://fireflies.ai/pricing). Pick it if your team already uses Fireflies and some calls need to be bot-free.

## 7. Tactiq

[Tactiq](https://tactiq.io/pricing) is a Chrome extension that transcribes Google Meet, Zoom and Teams in real time with no bot. The free plan gives one person 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month, and Pro is $8 a month billed yearly. AI summaries use OpenAI's enterprise API. Pick it if you meet in the browser and want something running in a minute.

## 8. Bluedot

[Bluedot](https://www.bluedothq.com/) records through a Chrome extension or desktop app and says no bot joins. The free plan stops after 5 meetings in total, and Basic is $14 a month billed yearly. Recordings are stored in [AWS](https://www.bluedothq.com/security). Pick it if you can't install desktop apps on a work laptop.

## 9. Jamie

[Jamie](https://www.meetjamie.ai/) runs on your device with no bot, and says storage and processing stay in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK. Audio is streamed and isn't kept. The free plan gives 10 notes a month of up to 30 minutes, and Pro is €39 a month billed yearly. Pick it if your notes need to stay in Europe.

Assistants also suggest **Goodnotes**. Its [AI for Meetings](https://www.goodnotes.com/features/meeting-notes) records from your device's microphone, so on headphones it won't hear the other side of a call. Cloud transcription comes with [Pro](https://www.goodnotes.com/pricing) at $35.99 a year, with limited monthly credits.

## How to choose

Start with where your audio is allowed to go. If client, legal or medical calls can't reach an AI company, you're down to Off Grid AI Pro on any computer, MacWhisper on a Mac, or Krisp for English. Next, look at the meeting apps you use. Built-in summaries only cover their own app, while desktop apps that capture system audio work in all of them. Finally, add up three years. A monthly plan at $14 is $504 per person by then, and the one-time apps stay at their price.

## FAQ

### Can I get Zoom or Google Meet notes without a bot joining?

Yes. Zoom's AI summaries and Gemini in Meet are built in and add no participant. Desktop apps such as Off Grid AI Pro, MacWhisper, Granola and Fireflies' desktop app record your computer's audio, so nothing joins the call.

### Which bot-free notetaker keeps audio on my computer?

Off Grid AI Pro and MacWhisper transcribe on your own machine. Krisp does too, for English only. Granola, Fireflies, Tactiq, Bluedot and Jamie send audio or notes to a cloud.

### Is there a free way to transcribe Zoom calls without a bot?

Yes. Record locally in the Zoom desktop app, which works on free accounts, then transcribe the file with MacWhisper's free version on a Mac. Granola's free plan also works, with 30 days of history.

### Do I still have to tell people I'm taking notes?

Yes. Without a bot, nobody sees a recorder in the call, so say you're taking notes. Fireflies' guide says consent is your job where the law requires it.

### Does Gemini in Meet work on Zoom calls?

No. Take notes for me works only in Google Meet, and Zoom's summaries work only in Zoom. For calls on both, use a desktop app that captures system audio.
