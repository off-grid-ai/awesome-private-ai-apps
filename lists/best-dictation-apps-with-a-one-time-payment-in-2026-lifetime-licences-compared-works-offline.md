## Off Grid AI Pro

**What you get.** Click into any text box, hold **Option+Space** on a Mac or **Alt+Space** on Windows and Linux, talk, and let go. Your words land where the cursor was, transcribed by a model on your own computer. In **Voice settings** you choose **Hold**, **Toggle** or **Both**, set your own **Shortcut**, pick the **Engine** (Whisper or Qwen3-ASR) and the **Language**, add custom words, and turn on **Remove filler words** if you want the ums gone. **Auto-delete** clears recordings after 7, 30 or 90 days.

**What else the same payment covers.** Meetings recorded without a bot, a local memory of your screens and documents, actions you approve, Vault and Sync. Turn on **Feed to memory** and the people, projects and to-dos you mention in a dictated note join what Off Grid AI knows about your work.

**What you need.** Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) is free and open source. Dictation into other apps is Pro: $34.99 once for lifetime access, a limited-time price down from $119, or $7.99 a month. Macs need Apple silicon, and Linux needs x64 with Ubuntu 24.04 or newer. The speech model needs internet once to download.

**Best for.** Anyone who dictates on more than one computer, or on Windows or Linux, and wants one payment to cover it. ## Handy

**What you get.** A free, open-source dictation app under the MIT licence. Press the shortcut, speak, release, and Handy pastes the text into the app you're in. It runs Whisper and Parakeet models on your computer, with builds for Mac, Windows and Linux.

**Best for.** People who want dictation and nothing else, at no cost.

**The catch.** Its README says Wayland support on Linux is limited and needs extra tools such as `wtype` or `dotool`. On Windows it can't paste into apps running as administrator unless Handy runs as administrator too.

## VoiceInk

**What you get.** An open-source (GPL v3) dictation app that transcribes on your Mac and writes into any app. Its optional Cloud Enhancement sends only the text, never your voice. Every plan is a one-time purchase with a 14-day money-back guarantee.

**Best for.** One person on one Apple silicon Mac who wants the lowest paid price: $25 once.

**The catch.** Apple silicon Macs with macOS 14.4 or later only. No Windows or Linux, and the biggest plan covers three Macs.

## MacWhisper Pro

**What you get.** A Mac transcription app that also dictates system-wide. Pro is €64 once with lifetime updates. It also transcribes files and records meetings in Zoom, Teams and others. You can run local models or connect cloud providers with your own keys.

**Best for.** Mac users who want file and meeting transcripts as much as dictation, paid once.

**The catch.** Mac only on the desktop. Its pages list a separate iOS and iPad version.

## Superwhisper Pro lifetime

**What you get.** Dictation into any app on Mac, Windows and Linux, plus iOS and Android apps. The free plan already includes unlimited Whisper models. Pro adds cloud models, your own API keys, translation and file transcription, and its FAQ says one licence works on as many of your devices as you like.

**Best for.** People who dictate on their phone as much as their computer.

**The catch.** The lifetime plan is $249.99. Meetings are Mac only, and the Linux app needs Wayland and systemd.

**What is the cheapest one-time dictation app?**

Yes. The speech model runs on your computer, so after it downloads once you can dictate with Wi-Fi off. Dictation into other apps is part of Pro, on Mac (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux (x64, Ubuntu 24.04 or newer).
