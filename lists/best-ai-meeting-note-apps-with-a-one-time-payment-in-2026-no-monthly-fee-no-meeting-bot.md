## 1. Off Grid AI Pro: one payment, every computer you own

**What you get.** Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) is a free, open-source AI app. Pro adds **Meetings**. When a Zoom or Google Meet call starts, it records on its own: the far side's audio, your microphone and your screen. In a room, or on any other call, press **Record meeting**. When you stop, the screen shows **Transcribing on device…** while a Whisper model writes the transcript on your computer, and a local model writes the summary with the decisions and who owes what.

**After the call.** The **Speakers** list tells voices apart, and you click a speaker to give them a name. Press **Chat about this meeting** to ask what was agreed. Pick a **Transcription language** and a **Transcription model**, and save the transcript, the audio or the full video to your disk. Turn on **Keep recording when away** and the recording continues when you switch away from the call window.

**Price.** $34.99 once for lifetime access, a limited-time price down from $119, or $7.99 a month. One key covers up to 5 devices, and it works the same on Mac (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux (x64, Ubuntu 24.04 or newer). You download the speech model once, and after that the notes work with no internet.

**Best for.** Client, legal or deal calls you'd rather keep on your own machine, and anyone on Windows or Linux who wants one price. The same key also brings Pro's memory of your screens and mail, actions and God, a chief of staff that preps your meetings.

## 2. MacWhisper: a transcript tool for the Mac

MacWhisper's free version transcribes files and your microphone. Meeting recording is in [Pro, €64 per licence, paid once](https://macwhisper.com), with lifetime updates. It records Zoom, Teams, Webex, Skype, Chime and Discord, and its App Audio option captures any app on your Mac. Transcription runs on your Mac, and you can connect OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama or LM Studio for AI.

Pick it if you only use a Mac and want transcripts more than an assistant. It doesn't run on Windows or Linux. See [Off Grid AI vs MacWhisper](https://getoffgridai.co/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-macwhisper-2026-local-transcription-on-every-computer-not-just-mac/).

## 3. Talat: a lifetime licence, Mac and Windows

[Talat](https://talat.app) records both sides of a call when it starts on Zoom, Teams or Meet, with no bot. Transcripts and notes stay in a local database, and its built-in Qwen3.5-4B writes the summaries on your computer. It sells a yearly plan at $89, or a lifetime licence for $189 if you'd rather pay once. You get 10 free hours of recording first.

It needs macOS 15 on Apple silicon, or Windows 10 or newer, and there's no Linux build. At $189 it costs more than Off Grid AI Pro.

## 4. Anarlog: free, open source, local transcription

Anarlog started as Hyprnote, was renamed Char, and is now Anarlog. Its [free plan](https://anarlog.so/pricing) has unlimited on-device transcription, local recordings and bring-your-own API keys, with no bot in the call. Its own pricing page warns that "Local speech recognition does not make summaries local," so check where your summary model runs.

There's no one-time paid plan. Pro is $14 a person a month or $140 a year, for cloud transcription, sync and sharing. Its [download page](https://anarlog.so/download) offers Mac builds, and Windows and Linux builds in beta.

## 5. Meetily: free and open source, Mac and Windows

[Meetily's Community edition](https://meetily.ai/pricing) is free under the MIT licence and transcribes on your device with Whisper. It captures system audio, so it works with Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, Discord, Slack Huddles and Webex. Summaries use a local model through Ollama, or your own Claude, OpenAI or Groq key, which sends the transcript (not the audio) to that provider.

You download it for Mac or Windows; Linux is a waitlist. Its paid Pro is a subscription at $10 a user a month, billed yearly at $120.
